How to Play

• Each scene has a short intro, then a rhythm sequence.

• Numbers (1, 2, 3) appear one after another. Press the matching key or button while it’s lit.

• Good hits fill the Passion meter. Misses drain it.

• Fill the meter before the sequence ends to succeed and move on.

• Finish all five scenes to become the Prince of Porn.
